Privacy Policy

This is placeholder content for the demo and is not legal advice. Final wording should be reviewed before the site goes live.

1. Overview

Little Sparrow Tattoo respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or visit the studio, and how we use it.

2. What we collect

When you get in touch we may collect your name, phone number, email address and the details of your enquiry. Before a tattoo or piercing we also collect the health and consent information required to work safely.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, manage your booking, provide our services and meet our health and safety obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We don't share your details with third parties except where needed to deliver our service or where required by law. Any reference photos you send are used only to plan and discuss your design.

5. Storage & security

We keep your information secure and only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as required by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you and request corrections. Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have rights over your personal information — contact us to exercise them.

7. Contact

For any privacy question, call 027 522 7449 or reach us through the contact section of our site.
