The studio

A small Timaru studio that takes its time

Little Sparrow Tattoo is a private, easy-going studio in the middle of Timaru. We keep the day unhurried so you're never rushed in the chair — plenty of time to talk through your design, get comfortable, and get it right.

Whether it's your first tattoo or your fortieth, you'll get honest advice, a clean space, and artwork drawn for you rather than pulled off a wall of flash.

  • Custom artwork Designed and refined with you before the needle touches skin.
  • Strict hygiene Single-use needles, sealed inks and a properly sterile setup.
  • Judgement-free & relaxed A calm studio where questions are welcome and nerves are normal.
